Third-party notices for PixelForge Studio (portable build)
===========================================================

PixelForge itself has no third-party dependencies. Everything in the "app"
and "launcher" folders, and the scripts beside this file, is PixelForge code
and original PixelForge artwork under the MIT license (LICENSE in the "app"
folder).

This build includes one third-party program so that nothing has to be
installed:

Node.js
-------
The "runtime" folder holds the official Node.js runtime for this system,
downloaded from https://nodejs.org and included unmodified. BUILD-INFO.txt
names the exact version, the address it was downloaded from and its SHA-256.

Node.js is distributed under the MIT license and contains further
third-party components of its own (among them V8, libuv, OpenSSL, ICU and
zlib). The complete license text for Node.js and for those components is
NODE-LICENSE.txt in the "runtime" folder.
