How it was made

Recipes, not bitmaps

The island is 29 PixelForge recipes: blob autotiles for sand and grass composed from hand-drawn 8×8 quarter symbols, water and surf animated by palette cycling, a keeper compiled from pose parts with sword and foot markers, and props drawn as palette grids.

Light from the art

The lighthouse, cottage and lamps ship aligned normal and emissive atlases; the sunflint has an emissive pass. At dusk and night the game composites colour × banded point lights + emission per pixel at 240×160, so the glow stays pixel-sharp.

Honest limits

Some things PixelForge could not express were handled in code: sprite pivots, glyph widths, tilemaps and the rotating beam. They are listed in the findings report alongside the bugs found while building this.
